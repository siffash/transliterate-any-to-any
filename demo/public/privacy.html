Privacy Policy

Effective October 19, 2026

This policy covers the Transliterate app for Android and iOS and the website transliterate-any-to-any.js.org, both made by Anton Sivashtenko (“I”, “me”) as a demo of the open-source transliterate-any-to-any library.

In short: I don't collect, store or share any personal data. There are no accounts, ads, analytics or tracking. Your text is transliterated on your device and is never sent to me.

Your text

The text you type, paste or dictate is transliterated on your device, by code that comes with the app (or that the website downloads to your browser). It isn't sent to me or to anyone else, and the app doesn't keep it after you close it.

Settings saved on your device

The app and the website remember the languages you picked and the light/dark mode, in your device's local storage, so that they're the same the next time. They never leave your device, and they're deleted when you uninstall the app or clear the website's data in your browser.

Voice input

Voice input is optional. The microphone permission is asked for only when you tap the microphone button, and the microphone is used only while voice input is on.

Your speech is turned into text by the speech recognition service of your device or browser: Apple's speech recognition on iPhone and iPad, the speech recognition service of your Android device (usually Google's), or your browser's on the website. Depending on the device and the language, that service may process the audio on its provider's servers, under the provider's privacy policy (Apple, Google). I never receive the audio or the text.

You can turn off the microphone permission at any time in your device's settings.

Clipboard

The app writes the transliteration to the clipboard only when you tap the copy button. It never reads your clipboard.

Links

The links at the bottom of the app (source code, npm and the article about the library) and of the website (also sponsoring) open other websites, such as GitHub, npm and WordPress, whose own privacy policies apply.

The website

In addition to the above, the website:

  • is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may log visitors' IP addresses for security (see GitHub's privacy statement);
  • loads its fonts from Google Fonts and parts of the transliteration data from the jsDelivr CDN, which receive your IP address and browser details when they send the files, as with any website;
  • keeps your text and languages in the page address, so that you can share a link to a transliteration: if you open or share such a link, the text is part of the address that your browser sends to GitHub Pages.

The app doesn't use any of these services: it contains everything it needs.

Children

Since no personal data is collected, the app and the website can be used by people of any age.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.

Contact

For any question about this policy, please open an issue on GitHub.
